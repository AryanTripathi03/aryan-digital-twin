# Asset Guide

## Portrait

- Recommended source: at least 1200 × 1600 px, sharp face, even light, uncluttered background
- Current asset: 960 × 1280 JPEG, 316 KB
- Current crop: compact 3:4 identity card, `object-position: 50% 39%`
- Keep the face and upper torso visible; do not create a giant hero portrait
- Future exports: AVIF and WebP at 320, 480 and 720 px widths
- Never generate or fabricate Aryan's face

## Project screenshots

For each flagship project capture:

- Desktop overview at 1440 × 900
- Tablet at 1024 × 768
- Mobile at 390 × 844 where applicable
- One architecture or data-flow view
- One evidence/detail view
- Synthetic or redacted data only

## Demo video

- 20-second teaser
- 60–90 second captioned walkthrough
- Optional full technical demonstration
- 1080p master, compressed H.264/AV1 web version, poster image and transcript
- No autoplay audio

## Certificates

- Original PDF
- 1200 px preview image
- Title, issuer, date, credential identifier and verification URL
- Confirm public-display rights and remove unnecessary personal identifiers
